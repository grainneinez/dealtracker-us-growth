01 / US expansion roadmap

30 / 60 / 90 days.
A focused US expansion.

Build a stronger US presence through law-firm relationships, useful thought leadership, targeted events and partnerships—with clear weekly follow-through.

30 daysListen & position

Build the US foundation.

  • Review the buyer journey, website messaging and demo handoff.
  • Agree the first US buyer segment and target account list.
  • Map relationships and conduct initial discovery conversations.
  • Build a US message framework and content calendar.

Checkpoint: A focused audience, baseline metrics and a shared growth brief.

60 daysActivate & connect

Put the strategy in market.

  • Begin targeted introductions and structured follow-up.
  • Publish practical content for deal teams and innovation buyers.
  • Convene a small NYC roundtable, subject to interest and budget.
  • Support demos and explore relevant partnership opportunities.

Checkpoint: Buyer feedback, qualified conversations and early channel signals.

90 daysMeasure & sharpen

Repeat what earns traction.

  • Assess which messages and channels generate meaningful interest.
  • Refine objections, demo narratives and follow-up materials.
  • Document a repeatable event-to-content-to-demo process.
  • Recommend the next quarter’s US priorities.

Checkpoint: An evidence-led growth playbook and a clear next-quarter plan.

Measure: qualified conversations, demo progression, engaged target accounts, inbound sources and partnership next steps. Numerical targets would be agreed after the baseline review.
